Clipper

A personal tool for turning long videos into short vertical clips and publishing them to your own YouTube channel.

What it does

Clipper runs on your own computer. It takes a source video, finds highlight moments, reframes them to a vertical (9:16) format with captions, and — when you choose to — uploads the finished clips to your own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Google account access

Clipper connects to your YouTube account through Google OAuth only to:

  • Upload videos you create to your own channel;
  • Read basic information and public statistics about your own channel and videos, to show you performance reports.

It does not access anyone else's account and does not post anywhere without your action.

Privacy

Clipper is a single-user application. Your Google sign-in stays on your own machine, and the developer does not collect or receive your data. See the full Privacy Policy.
